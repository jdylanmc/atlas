# Use ATX-Style Headings

Use ATX-style (#) headings rather than Setext-style (underline) headings, since underline style is ambiguous about heading level.[^s1]

[^s1]: [[.atlas/sources/google-markdown-style-guide]] Quoted span "Headings with `=` or `-` underlines can be annoying to maintain and don't fit".
