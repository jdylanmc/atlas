# A Review Finding Is Resolved Only by the Adversarial Corpus

## Active truths

- `truth:adversarial-corpus-resolves-findings` A durable review finding is resolved only after tests/adversarial/ carries a reject or accept case that exercises it; a finding addressed only in prose or in a non-adversarial test remains open.
