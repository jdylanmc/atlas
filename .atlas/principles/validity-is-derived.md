# Validity Is Always Derived

## Active truths

- `truth:validity-is-derived` Validity is derived by Atlas SDK itself and is never asserted by a caller; a caller's claim that its input is valid carries no weight against the SDK's own derivation.

## Amendments

### 1 - 2026-08-24

Added `truth:validity-is-derived` under Maintainer approval. Established as the general form of the construction-over-guarding and derivation principles above, applied to caller-supplied validity claims specifically.
